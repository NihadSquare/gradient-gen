# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), in short form.

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, background, identity, experience, nationality, appearance, religion or orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intentions.
- Respect other people's time, opinions and experience.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Say sorry when you get it wrong and learn from it.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, threats, harassment or discrimination
- Sexualised language or attention
- Publishing someone's private information
- Spam, and anything else that would be unwelcome in a professional setting

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@NihadSquare](https://github.com/NihadSquare)) may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests and other contributions, and may block people who break these rules. To report a problem privately, use *Security → Report a vulnerability* on the repository page and start your message with "Conduct report", or contact the maintainer through the contact details on the GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence.
