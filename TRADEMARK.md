**Gradient Gen**, **Nihad Square**, the Gradient Gen wordmark (the blue "Gradient Gen" lettering), the **NS** mark and the look of the Gradient Gen interface are brand marks of AK Nihad (Nihad Square). They are used as unregistered marks (™). This policy tells you how you may and may not use them. The source code licence is in [LICENSE](LICENSE); this policy covers the *names and the brand*.

## You may

- Write about Gradient Gen: reviews, tutorials, articles, videos and social posts that call it by its real name and link to the official website or repository.
- Show screenshots of the official app in such articles and videos.
- Link to the official website, repository and releases.
- Say that your pictures were "made with Gradient Gen".

## You may not

- Use "Gradient Gen", "Nihad Square" or a name that is easy to confuse with them in the name of a product, app, website, domain, repository, package, store listing, social media account or company.
- Use the logos, wordmark, NS mark or the artwork in `public/assets` and `docs`, or imitate the interface so that people believe your product is the official one or is made, approved or endorsed by me.
- Publish a copy, fork or re-packaged build under these names or with these logos.
- Remove the notices that say who made Gradient Gen and where the official version is.

## Forks

A fork on GitHub that exists only to prepare a contribution is fine. Any other copy must not present itself as Gradient Gen (see LICENSE section 2).

## Reporting a copy or a misuse

Open an issue in the official repository, or use *Security → Report a vulnerability* and start your message with "Brand report". Please include the address of the copy and a screenshot.
